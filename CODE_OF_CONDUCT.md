# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and
constructive for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Provide constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Accept differing viewpoints and respond professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the
contact information on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be
reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or
remove content and restrict participation when necessary.
